A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Everything you need to get started, in plain words.

Who is Pinglo for?+

Pinglo is for small businesses that want to keep customer conversations personal and follow-ups organized.

Can I start for free?+

Yes. The Free Starter plan includes up to 15 active contacts and the core follow-up tools.

Can I follow up through WhatsApp?+

Yes. You can open a WhatsApp follow-up directly from a customer's record.

Does Premium Pro renew automatically?+

No. Pro is prepaid and does not auto-renew. Paid upgrades are still being prepared, so no payment is collected at the moment.
