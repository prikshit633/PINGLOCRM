How prepaid Pro will work

Will Pro renew automatically?

No. You choose when to top up; there will be no automatic monthly deduction.

How long does access last?

A monthly top-up adds 30 days. The yearly pack adds 365 days to your Pro validity.

What happens when Pro ends?

Your workspace returns to Free limits. Your saved customer records remain in your account.
